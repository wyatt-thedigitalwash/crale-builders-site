META: Crale Builders, located in Sidney, Ohio, is a professional custom builder specializing in residential, commercial and industrial construction throughout Midwest Ohio including Shelby, Miami, Champaign, Logan and Auglaize counties. We offer total project development services including design and drafting services, construction management and general contracting.
