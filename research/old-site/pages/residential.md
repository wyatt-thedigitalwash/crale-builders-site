TITLE: Crale Builders – Custom Residential Builder & General Contractor
META:  Located in Sidney, Ohio, Crale Builders is a professional custom residential builder serving Midwest Ohio including Shelby, Miami, Champaign, Logan and Auglaize counties. We offer total project development services including design and drafting services, construction management and general contracting for new home construction, additions, renovations and small projects.

## TEXT
Residential Services
From concept to completion, Crale Builders offers total project development services including design and drafting services, construction management and general contracting on a professional, yet personal basis.
Our philosophy is one of complete identity with our client’s interests at all times. We work closely with you during each phase of the design and construction process to ensure a similar vision with common expectations. We will sit down as many times as it takes to enable you to make effective judgments regarding design, costs, schedules and financing. We have extensive knowledge and experience in the construction business and can provide professional, expert advice on all your building concerns.
You can rest assured that Crale Builders has your best interests in mind. We work hard to ensure your project is completed on time, within budget and to your complete satisfaction.
New Home Construction
Crale Builders is committed to building quality homes with meticulous craftsmanship and custom designs that reflect your needs, your style and your personality. We understand that building a custom home may be one of the largest investments you will ever make and we want it to be an exciting and rewarding experience for you. Our top priority is your complete satisfaction through effective communication, superior service and quality work.
Additions/Renovations
When done the right way, additions and renovations not only increase your living or work space and make it more comfortable and functional, but also increase the resale value of your property. Whether it’s expanding square footage, building another story, changing the exterior or making changes within, Crale Builders has the knowledge and experience to make valuable improvements to your existing home or business to fit your budget and your needs.
