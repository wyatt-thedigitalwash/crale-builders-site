About Crale Builders
Crale Builders was established in 1995 by Dale Bensman and Craig Kuck. We are a professional custom builder specializing in residential and commercial construction throughout Midwest Ohio including Shelby, Miami, Champaign, Logan and Auglaize counties.
From concept to completion, we offer total development services including design and drafting services, construction management and general contracting on a professional, yet personal basis.

Quality You Can Count On
Whether your project represents your home or your business, Crale Builders has the knowledge and experience to ensure your project is a success. We are committed to achieving the highest level of craftsmanship, quality and productivity. We utilize a staff of qualified and experienced design and construction professionals and choose subcontractors and material suppliers who share in our commitment to customer satisfaction.
It is our goal to make the building process a trouble-free experience while meeting your critical production deadlines and budget. We are concerned with doing things right the first time and believe it is just as important to ensure our customer's satisfaction after construction as it is during. That’s why all construction is under warranty for a period of one year.
