Dedicated to Excellence Since 1995
Crale Builders, Inc. specializes in custom residential and commercial construction throughout Midwest Ohio; including Shelby, Miami, Champaign, Logan and Auglaize counties. We are an experienced and knowledgeable builder, dedicated to providing the highest level of craftsmanship, quality and productivity. We work hard to ensure that your projects are completed on time and on budget and that you are completely satisfied with the results. We understand that this may be one of the largest investments you will ever make and we want it to be an exciting and rewarding experience for you!
